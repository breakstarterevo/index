November 1983 Race Watch: Palace Control, Tier 3 Crowds The Door

Palace and Brighton hold the Tier 2 promotion line, Barcelona lead a packed Tier 3 chase, and Tier 1 already has Bayern sweating.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Sim 1

Race watch starts with the line, not the noise. Tier 1 sends two down, Tier 2 sends two up and one down, and Tier 3 has one promotion seat. November already made each line readable.

Tier 1 Relegation Line
Bayern Are Below It, AC Sparta Praha And FL Fart Are On It
Line: AC Sparta Praha, 2-6 | Slots: 2

Bayern Munich are the early shock because 1-6 puts them below the line even though the minus-28 differential is not hopeless. AC Sparta Praha and FL Fart are both 2-6, but they are getting there in different ways: AC Sparta Praha have the heavier damage at minus-124, while FL Fart are sliding on four straight losses. Chelsea are only one game clear, but their plus-8 margin gives them the first safety argument.

Tier 2 Promotion Line
Palace Control The Top Seat, Brighton Hold The Second
Line: Brighton, 5-2 | Slots: 2

Crystal Palace are not merely above the line; they are trying to make the line about everyone else. The 9-1 record and plus-157 differential give them full control. Brighton sit second at 5-2, with enough margin to make the place feel earned. Juventus are the real pressure team one game back, while Aston Villa need to stop the two-game slide before the early 4-4 record starts reading like drift.

Tier 2 Relegation Line
Ajax Sit In Trouble, United Are Not Safe Enough
Line: Ajax, 1-7 | Slots: 1

Ajax are the team on the trapdoor, and the numbers are not subtle: 1-7, minus-100, six straight losses. Manchester United are one game better but almost as cold, carrying their own six-game skid and a minus-90 margin. Marseille's four-game winning streak gives them breathing room, though the minus-51 differential says they should not get too comfortable.

Tier 3 Promotion Line
Barcelona Lead, But Monaco And PSG Are Already In The Frame
Line: Barcelona, 6-2 | Slots: 1

Tier 3 is the cleanest race and the most annoying one. Barcelona are 6-2 with the strongest line position, Monaco are 6-1 and rising, and PSG are 6-3 with enough direct-result bite to stay in the argument. With only one promotion seat, this is less a race than a weekly stress test.

Palace have control. Tier 3 has compression. Bayern have a problem that is too early to dismiss.

The next sim matters most for teams one result from a new label. Brighton can make the second promotion seat feel real. Monaco can make Barcelona defend every inch. Bayern need to stop living on the wrong side of the Tier 1 line.
