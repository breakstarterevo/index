December 1984 MVP Race: Winning Breaks The Scoring Tie

The league has no shortage of thirty-point scorers; the second ballot asks whose production changes the meaning of his team's season.

Damon CrossDecember 1984

Kiki Vandeweghe scores more than everybody. Joe Barry Carroll blocks more shots than every serious scorer. Larry Nance has been magnificent while Aston Villa have fallen through the floor. An MVP ballot is where those facts stop living separately.

Availability is set at sixty percent of team games. Production begins the case; efficiency, team performance and the degree to which a player explains that performance finish it. December did not replace all three leaders. It made the objections beneath them louder.

Tier 1 Ballot
The leader: Tom Chambers
  1. 1Tom Chambers, AFC Richmond
    30.8 PTS | 5.8 REB | 64.9 TS% | +10.4
    STAYED NO. 1. The scoring dipped slightly and the team still reached 13-4. Chambers remains the best intersection of volume, efficiency and command. Carroll is closer; Richmond's lead keeps Tom first.
  2. 2Joe Barry Carroll, AC Milan
    30.9 PTS | 9.5 REB | 3.8 BLK | 25.8 PER
    STAYED NO. 2. This is the strongest individual two-way file: elite interior scoring, rebounding and rim protection for a 10-6 club. One sustained Milan push would make him the favorite.
  3. 3Kiki Vandeweghe, Brighton
    36.6 PTS | 6.6 REB | 66.0 TS% | -1.4
    STAYED NO. 3. The scoring title is his. The even team record and negative on-court margin are not. Kiki owns the loudest statistical case and the clearest burden of context.
  4. 4Magic Johnson, AFC Richmond
    18.6 PTS | 8.2 AST | 68.7 TS% | +13.9
    NEW TO TOP FIVE. Magic's value hides in cost: he creates, scores with absurd efficiency and owns the ballot's best plus-minus. The hesitation is sharing Richmond's credit with Chambers.
  5. 5Jim Paxson, Chelsea
    30.2 PTS | 3.3 AST | 1.9 STL | +0.2
    NEW TO TOP FIVE. Paxson gives Chelsea elite scoring and helps explain the positive differential. Seventh place explains why the case stops here.
Tier 2 Ballot
The leader: Kelly Tripucka
  1. 1Kelly Tripucka, Paris Saint-Germain
    33.3 PTS | 5.6 AST | 66.7 TS% | +14.3
    STAYED NO. 1. Real passed PSG in the standings; nobody passed Tripucka as the tier's cleanest offensive engine. The margin, efficiency and twelve-win context remain complete.
  2. 2Sidney Moncrief, Real Madrid
    27.7 PTS | 5.5 REB | 66.7 TS% | +12.7
    STAYED NO. 2, CLOSING FAST. The leader of an 8-0 month now owns the best team argument. Tripucka's larger creation load is the only remaining separation.
  3. 3Johnny Moore, Atletico Madrid
    20.5 PTS | 14.1 AST | 3.3 STL | 42.0 MIN
    UP 2. Fourteen assists and three steals a night are not decoration; they are Atletico's system. The losing record keeps an extraordinary guard season below the promotion stars.
  4. 4Larry Nance, Aston Villa
    32.2 PTS | 9.0 REB | 2.3 BLK | 61.3 TS%
    DOWN 1. Nance has an MVP stat line and a seven-game team losing streak. That is not proof he failed. It is proof individual dominance has limits in this ballot.
  5. 5Cliff Robinson, Ajax
    25.8 PTS | 8.6 REB | 59.9 TS% | +4.1
    NEW TO TOP FIVE. Ajax's rise needs representation. Robinson gives it efficient wing scoring, rebounding and a positive margin across eighteen games.
Tier 3 Ballot
The leader: Paul Pierce
  1. 1Paul Pierce, Barcelona
    28.3 PTS | 5.7 REB | 2.0 STL | 67.1 TS%
    STAYED NO. 1. Pierce combines the best scoring-efficiency line on the promotion leader with enough defense and playmaking to avoid becoming a specialist's case.
  2. 2Otis Birdsong, Barcelona
    25.6 PTS | 4.0 AST | 1.9 STL | +6.5
    STAYED NO. 2. Two Barcelona players atop one ballot is uncomfortable. So is ignoring the second-best explanation for why Barcelona still hold first.
  3. 3World B. Free, Inter Milan
    25.6 PTS | 20.8 PER | 30.1 MIN | +5.8
    NEW TO TOP FIVE. Free produces starter-level offense in barely thirty minutes for the tier's strongest margin team. More floor time could turn efficiency into a direct challenge.
  4. 4Isiah Thomas, Valencia
    21.9 PTS | 9.9 AST | 1.7 STL | 62.6 TS%
    UP 1. Isiah's scoring and creation organize a Valencia team with a positive season margin. The 8-7 record is the ceiling on the current ballot, not on his play.
  5. 5Andre Drummond, Monaco
    21.0 PTS | 12.0 REB | 2.2 BLK | 18 GP
    DOWN 2. The rebounding and rim protection remain elite. Monaco's 2-6 month turns durability into a defense of the spot rather than a route upward.
Tripucka has the best offensive case. Moncrief has the best December. Chambers still has the cleanest answer when production and winning are forced into the same sentence.

Joe Barry Carroll is the ballot's closest challenger. Sidney Moncrief is its biggest riser without moving a slot. The next month will test whether team control can outweigh Tripucka's volume and whether Inter's rise finally breaks Barcelona's two-man hold on Tier 3.
