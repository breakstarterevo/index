November 1984 Tier 2 Power Rankings: PSG Turned Promotion Into A Chase

PSG own the best profile, Real Madrid own the second promotion place, and Aston Villa have made the gap between those facts uncomfortably small.

Nina ValeNovember 1984

Tier 2 already has a hierarchy, but not peace. Paris Saint-Germain are 6-1 with a plus-166 differential. Real Madrid are a game back. Aston Villa sit another half-game behind and finished November with three straight wins.

This is the opening Season 4 board, so every placement is a new ranking based only on the first regular-season results and the evidence they produced.
