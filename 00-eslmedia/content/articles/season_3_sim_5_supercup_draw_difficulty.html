Supercup Draw Difficulty: Sporting Took The Tier Tax

Using only the Supercup regular-season schedule, tier-adjusted opponent strength puts Arsenal, Ajax and Inter Milan on the hardest roads.

Nina ValeSupercup regular-season draw

This is not a preseason reading. Preseason games are excluded. It is also not a raw win-percentage list, because a bad Champions League team should not be treated as easier than a good Conference League team by default. The ranking below weights tier first, then record inside the tier: CLB opponents carry the highest base difficulty, ELB opponents the next, and ECL opponents the lowest, with opponent win percentage breaking ties and adding shape.

Hardest Tier-Adjusted Draws

1. Arsenal: Tier-adjusted score 2.057. Opponent record 164-131, with a 5/2/1 tier split. Sporting drew AC Milan, Chelsea, Monaco, AFC Richmond, Marseille, AC Sparta Praha, Benfica, and Aston Villa. Five CLB opponents is the heaviest tax in the field, and it changes the story completely from the raw-record view.

2. Ajax: Score 1.987. Opponent record 181-114, tier split 4/3/1. Ajax still grade as brutally treated even after the tier correction because they drew Benfica, PSG, Richmond, Milan, Palace, Villa, Atletico and FL Fart. That is both top-heavy and record-heavy.

3. Inter Milan: Score 1.911. Opponent record 158-137, tier split 5/1/2. Inter's raw opponent record is not as scary as Real Madrid's, but five CLB opponents make the route much harder than the win percentage alone suggests.

4. FL Fart: Score 1.895. Opponent record 153-141, tier split 5/1/2. Milan, AC Sparta Praha, Benfica, Ajax, Richmond and Chelsea give the draw a Champions League spine, even if some of those teams are carrying uneven records.

5. Barcelona: Score 1.744. Opponent record 148-151, tier split 3/4/1. Barcelona do not face the best raw record, but the draw is dense: Benfica, United, Palace, PSG, Bayern, Juventus, Marseille and Real Madrid.

Easiest Tier-Adjusted Draws

1. AC Sparta Praha: Score 0.982. Opponent record 143-152, tier split 1/2/5. PSG and Inter are nasty, but five ECL opponents drag the total difficulty down. For a Tier 1 club trying to find stability, this is the draw you would choose before looking too closely at PSG.

2. Brighton: Score 1.067. Opponent record 131-165, tier split 1/3/4. Inter is the spike, Bayern adds name value, but Brighton's Supercup schedule gives the Tier 2 leader a comparatively manageable run.

3. Monaco: Score 1.127. Opponent record 148-146, tier split 1/3/4. The raw opponent record looks almost even, but only one CLB opponent keeps the tier-adjusted number light.

4. Aston Villa: Score 1.208. Opponent record 136-161, tier split 1/4/3. Villa see a lot of middle-tier traffic and only one Champions League opponent, which makes the draw easier than the club-name list first appears.

5. Benfica: Score 1.212. Opponent record 136-158, tier split 2/2/4. Benfica face Barcelona, Villa, City, Inter, Ajax, FL Fart, Arsenal and Bayern. The two CLB games keep it honest, but four ECL opponents soften the total.

Why The Correction Matters

Record-only difficulty had Real Madrid at the top because their opponents are a combined 183-114. The tier-adjusted view drops them to eighth because that route includes three ECL opponents. That does not make Madrid's schedule easy; PSG, Inter, Barcelona and Richmond still make it dangerous. It does mean the draw is not as harsh as Sporting's five-CLB slate or Ajax's four-CLB, three-ELB mix.

The clean takeaway is this: the hardest Supercup draw belongs to the teams paying a tier tax, not simply the teams facing the prettiest combined record. Arsenal, Ajax, Inter and FL Fart got the roughest version of the board. AC Sparta Praha, Brighton, Monaco, Villa and Benfica got the most forgiving version.
