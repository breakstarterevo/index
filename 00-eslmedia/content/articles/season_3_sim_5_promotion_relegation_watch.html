March 1984 Race Watch: Brighton Take The Line, Madrid Lose The Floor

The promotion seats are clearer than the survival fights, but March made the trapdoors feel much less abstract.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Sim 5

Race watch starts with the rules. Tier 1 sends two clubs down. Tier 2 sends two clubs up and one club down. Tier 3 sends one club up. Monthly form matters only after the actual standings line is established, and the actual lines are now getting sharper.

Tier 1 Relegation: Madrid And Sheffield Are Below It

AFC Richmond are not the race. The race is at the bottom, where Real Madrid sit seventh at 11-26 and Sheffield United sit eighth at 10-27. Those are the two relegation places. FL Fart, at 14-22, are only three wins above Madrid and four above Sheffield, so they are not safe enough to relax.

March made the pressure worse because Madrid went 1-6 and Sheffield went 1-7. Bayern Munich's 15-22 record is not pretty, but a 4-3 month gave them enough distance to breathe. The bottom two did not get that courtesy.

Tier 2 Promotion: Brighton And Palace Hold The Seats

Brighton are first at 27-10. Crystal Palace are second at 26-12. That is the promotion line, and it remains a two-club line until someone actually moves it. Aston Villa's 5-2 March matters, but their 19-18 overall record leaves them well behind the top two. Juventus are 20-18, which keeps them in the conversation without making them live on the line.

The proper reading is not "Villa are promoted by momentum." The proper reading is that Villa improved their pressure while Brighton and Palace still own the actual seats.

Tier 2 Relegation: United Are Last, Atletico Are The Alarm

Manchester United are last at 10-27, which is the relegation place. Atletico Madrid, though, are now the club that looks most unstable above the trapdoor: 15-22 overall, 1-6 in March, minus-96 for the sim. That gap is still five wins, so the table says United are in deeper trouble. The form says Atletico should not like the sound behind them.

Tier 3 Promotion: PSG Lead, Inter Chase

Paris Saint-Germain are first at 30-8. Inter Milan are second at 27-9, two games back by the standings table. Barcelona and Monaco remain relevant at 23 wins, but the one-promotion rule turns relevance into a hard climb. Monaco's 6-3 month keeps them mentioned; the negative margin keeps the mention qualified.

That is the state of the races after March: Brighton and Palace control the upward seats, PSG control the single ECL door, and the loudest panic belongs to clubs that spent the month making the standings look less forgiving.
