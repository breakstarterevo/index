November 1984 Stock Report: Richmond And PSG Broke Away

Three clubs used November to change their status; three others discovered that one consoling win cannot repair a month of structural damage.

Damon CrossNovember 1984

Some records require interpretation. Richmond's does not. Six wins, one three-point loss, plus-144. That is not a nice month. That is a club announcing that Tier 1 has to come through them.

Stock Up
AFC Richmond

Trigger: A five-game closing streak. Evidence: Milan by 33, Palace by 27, FL Fart by 26. Consequence: Richmond are no longer a contender among contenders; they are the measuring stick. The inconvenient detail is FL Fart's 119-116 upset, proof that they can still be dragged into a close game.

Paris Saint-Germain

Trigger: Three early blowouts and a late win at Real Madrid. Evidence: 5-1 with a plus-134 differential. Consequence: PSG have made promotion the expected outcome. Villa's four-point win in Paris is the warning: late-game control remains less convincing than the first thirty minutes.

Inter Milan

Trigger: Three consecutive wins to finish. Evidence: 5-2 and plus-61, capped by 116-75 at Sporting. Consequence: Inter have moved from preseason afterthought to one-game promotion challenger. Next comes the difficult part—beating Barcelona or Tottenham rather than repeatedly punishing the bottom.

Stock Down
Arsenal

Trigger: One win in eight. Evidence: Minus-97 for November and a 41-point closing loss. Consequence: Chris Bosh's development is now happening inside losing conditions. The 98-84 win over United bought one evening of relief and disappeared the next night.

Manchester United

Trigger: Six straight losses after the Manchester derby upset. Evidence: 1-7 and minus-77. Consequence: United are already outside the meaningful promotion race. Beating City proves the roster can create one sharp night; it has not shown how to build a week.

Juventus

Trigger: Five losses before the closing recovery. Evidence: 2-5 and minus-85, including a 37-point loss at PSG. Consequence: Two late wins save Juventus from hopelessness, not from the bottom of Tier 2. If the streak continues, this verdict ages quickly. For now, the damage still outweighs the rescue.

Richmond and PSG created distance. Sporting and United created archaeology: every good possession now has to be excavated from the losses around it.
