January 1984 Race Watch: PSG And Inter Share The Tightest Line

Use the standings first: Valencia had the hot month, but the real ECL promotion fight is PSG and Inter tied at the top.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Sim 3 race watch

Race watch has to start with the table, not the loudest month. January made Valencia interesting, but not close enough to call them a promotion-line team. The true ECL line is much higher: Paris Saint-Germain and Inter Milan are both 18-5, with PSG holding the first listed spot and Inter level on record.

CLB Relegation
Drop spots: Bayern Munich 8-15 and Sheffield United 7-16

Sheffield are bottom by a game, while Bayern sit level with Real Madrid at 8-15 and remain inside the danger group. Real are not free; they are the nearest club above the line, and their L3 streak keeps the pressure alive. FL Fart are 10-13 and have more room than their January form suggested.

ELB Promotion
Promotion spots: Crystal Palace 20-5 and Brighton 15-7

Palace remain the control team. The live fight is second: Brighton are 15-7, Juventus are 14-8, and that is a one-game gap. Juventus' 6-2 January matters because it moved them close enough for the standings to care.

ELB Relegation
Drop spot: Manchester United 5-19

United are last and on a seven-game slide. Ajax are 6-17, close enough that this is still a race, but Marseille at 10-13 have a wider buffer than a monthly-form-only view would imply.

ECL Promotion
Promotion line: PSG 18-5 and Inter Milan 18-5 tied at the top

Only one ECL team goes up, and the top two are level. Monaco and Barcelona are both 3.5 games back, close enough to stay in the picture. Valencia are 10-13 and 8 games back, so their 7-2 January is a stock-up story, not a promotion-line story yet.

The correction is simple: January form tells us who is moving; the standings tell us who is actually on the line. Right now, the standings say Brighton-Juventus and PSG-Inter are the races that matter most.
