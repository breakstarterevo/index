Season 2 Preseason Tier 1 Power Rankings: Richmond Set The First Standard

Richmond's 5-1 opening is the clearest Tier 1 statement, while Real Madrid and AC Milan establish the early group most likely to press them.

Nina ValeSeason 2 - Preseason Sim 1

A preseason power board should measure readiness, not pretend to award standing points. Tier 1 gave a useful sample anyway: AFC Richmond produced the best record, best point differential and most complete collection of meaningful wins in the opening sim.

The first board rewards what teams have shown, while leaving room for how quickly preseason impressions can dissolve once November begins.

1. AFC Richmond - 5-1, plus-61

Richmond own the top spot because the evidence is broad rather than dependent on one result. They beat Bayern Munich by 22, handled Benfica and Atletico Madrid, and closed with a road win at Benfica. The loss to AC Milan prevents any sense of invulnerability, but it also gives Richmond a credible standard against which to refine the next run.

2. Real Madrid - 4-2, plus-40

Real Madrid lost at AC Sparta Praha, then later answered by beating the same opponent by 23. Their 142-127 victory over AC Milan is the most striking offensive result among the nearest Richmond challengers. The question is not whether their best performance belongs at this level; it is whether the defensive variation settles down.

3. AC Milan - 4-2, plus-32

Milan have the most valuable single scalp on the board after winning 124-113 at Richmond. They also beat Crystal Palace twice, which is less revealing given Palace's difficult start. The 15-point defeat at Real Madrid leaves them behind the first two, but a team capable of beating the current No. 1 cannot be treated as ornamental depth.

4. Atletico Madrid - 3-3, plus-32

No team on this board illustrates the value of margins better than Atletico. A level record normally suggests the middle, yet their plus-32 differential came with convincing wins over Benfica and Crystal Palace. They sit fourth because the losses to Richmond and Real Madrid indicate where their early ceiling still needs proof.

5. AC Sparta Praha - 3-3, minus-19

AC Sparta Praha produced a real win over Real Madrid and avoided a losing record. They also lost the return meeting by 23 and were sufficiently uneven to finish with a negative differential. That is a credible fifth-place opening: dangerous on the right night, not yet convincing across the sim.

6. Benfica - 3-3, minus-25

Benfica began by beating Bayern Munich and finished close against Richmond, but the middle of the run was damaging. A 27-point loss at Atletico is the kind of preseason result that exposes a problem even when there is no table punishment. The record is acceptable; the shape of it is less comforting.

7. Bayern Munich - 2-4, minus-34

Bayern have enough stature that a poor preseason need not become a prediction. It does have to become evidence. They beat AC Sparta Praha, but defeats to Benfica and Richmond leave the leading concern clear: the top-end matchups arrived too quickly for them in Sim 1.

8. Crystal Palace - 0-6, minus-87

There is no analytical trick that moves an 0-6 club off the bottom. Palace competed closely at AC Sparta Praha, losing by two, but otherwise spent the opening sim absorbing separation. November can reset the consequences. Before then, the priority is finding a version of the team that can stop a poor sequence from becoming an identity.

Richmond have the first claim on Tier 1 readiness. Palace have the first urgent repair job. Everything between them is still open.

This is a preseason board, and it should be read accordingly. Richmond begin at the front because they earned it in the available games. The official season will decide whether that early clarity survives contact with stakes.
