December 1985 Tier 2 Power Rankings: Ajax Lead, Villa Apply Heat

The division's cleanest full-season profile meets its hottest team, squeezing Inter and FL Fart into a promotion chase with no cheap route upward.

Nina ValeDecember 1985

Aston Villa won every December game. Ajax still lead this board. The distinction is not resistance to new evidence; it is the reason a trust ranking uses the whole season before asking what the latest month changed.

Ajax are 11-3 with a plus-181 margin. Villa are 12-5, plus-93 and on a ten-game winning streak. Both occupy the promotion places. The larger change is below them, where two strong teams now need extraordinary months merely to recover the ground December removed.

Tier 2 Trust Board — December 1985
  1. 1AjaxUP 2 FROM NO. 3
    11-3 overall | 5-1 December | +181 | W5 | Andre Iguodala

    Ajax own the tier's best winning percentage, scoring margin and current five-game streak. Andre Iguodala scores 27.6 points on 66.2 percent true shooting with a plus-9.9 on-court margin; Maurice Cheeks adds 9.9 assists and 2.8 steals. FL Fart beat them by 17, the month's lone objection. Ajax answered by winning the next five.

  2. 2Aston VillaUP 2 FROM NO. 4
    12-5 | 8-0 December | +93 | W10 | Larry Nance

    The perfect month was not a collection of escapes. Villa won by 13.5 points a night and finished with a 27-point result over AC Sparta Praha. Larry Nance's 30 points, 11.5 rebounds and 2.2 blocks are the star case; seven wins after the one-point opener are the team case. Ajax remain first on full-season dominance. One more Villa month makes that distinction impossible to preserve.

  3. 3FL FartDOWN 2 FROM NO. 1
    11-7 | 4-3 December | +111 | L2 | Larry Drew

    FL Fart beat Ajax and Inter, direct evidence that the top two are reachable. Larry Drew's 10.1 assists keep an elite home offense organized. The problem is increasingly geographic: 9-1 at home, 2-6 away, then two losses to close the month. Third reflects a promotion-quality ceiling attached to a schedule-dependent floor.

  4. 4Inter MilanDOWN 2 FROM NO. 2
    10-6 | 4-5 December | +101 | W1 | World B. Free

    Inter remain balanced home and away and still own a plus-101 margin. December weakened the case without breaking it. Ajax scored 140 in Milan, FL Fart scored 136, and a 4-5 month placed Inter two games off the line. World B. Free gives them enough scoring to respond. The next board needs the response, not its possibility.

  5. 5MarseilleUP 1 FROM NO. 6
    6-8 | 3-4 December | -38 | W2 | Robert Parish

    Marseille move because the lower half offered even less stability. Robert Parish averages 27.9 points and 14.4 rebounds, and closing wins over Atletico and FL Fart created a credible January platform. Three close December games suggest the margin can move quickly. Five games to the promotion lead explain why this is recovery, not a chase.

  6. 6AC Sparta PrahaDOWN 1 FROM NO. 5
    7-10 | 3-5 December | -140 | L1 | Steve Johnson

    Steve Johnson produces 35.6 points and 11.3 rebounds on 63.3 percent true shooting. AC Sparta Praha still went minus-93 in December. A win over Inter demonstrates how large Johnson can make one night; a 1-7 road record shows why it has not become a season. They are clear of relegation, but the star's performance is carrying too much structural weight.

  7. 7Atletico MadridUP 1 FROM NO. 8
    4-10 | 3-4 December | -73 | L1 | Johnny Moore

    Atletico nearly balanced December and Johnny Moore continues to bend possessions with 11.4 assists and 3.6 steals. That is meaningful improvement from November's one-win opening. It is also only four total wins. Three games of relegation clearance provide room; a 1-5 home record prevents comfort.

  8. 8Bayern MunichDOWN 1 FROM NO. 7
    2-14 | 0-8 December | -235 | L12 | Tree Rollins

    There is no analytical trick available. Bayern lost every December game by an average of 19.1 points, have lost twelve straight and remain 0-11 away. The roster contains useful players. The team has stopped converting usefulness into competitive games. Safety sits three games away and feels farther.

Board Notes
  • Control team: Ajax, with the strongest record-rate and margin.
  • Biggest riser: Aston Villa, perfect in December and now on ten straight.
  • Trust problem: FL Fart's 2-6 road record.
  • Panic team: Bayern Munich. No qualifier is required.

The top two can change order without changing the promotion picture. The bigger January question is whether Inter or FL Fart can interrupt the separation before Ajax and Villa make the race a private arrangement.
