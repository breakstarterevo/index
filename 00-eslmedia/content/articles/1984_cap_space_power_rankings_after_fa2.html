After FA2 Cap Space Power Rankings: Aston Villa Owns The Leverage

The league's largest wallet also belongs to one of its thinnest rotations, making Villa the club that can still change every remaining negotiation.

Nina Vale1984 Offseason - After FA2

Cap room is useful only when it intersects with a real need and a market capable of answering it. That distinction matters after FA2. Several clubs still have money; fewer have both the roster urgency and the remaining targets to turn that money into leverage.

Aston Villa has all three. Its $31.97 million in cap room is more than $10 million clear of the field, while a two-guard, one-big roster leaves no ambiguity about the work still required. Villa can outbid, split its room across positions or wait for the market to come down. No other club retains that many credible paths.

The Post-FA2 Leverage Board
RankClubWhy the room matters
  1. 1Aston Villa

    $31.97M cap room · $161.97M budget room · guard and frontcourt needs

    Villa can set the price for Rickey Green and still address its one-big frontcourt. The concern is timing: every first-wave signing made the remaining pool less forgiving.

  2. 2Juventus

    $21.84M cap room · $151.84M budget room · no acute positional hole

    The flexibility is enormous, but the need is optional. Juventus can wait for value, absorb a premium target or preserve room without damaging a functional roster.

  3. 3AC Sparta Praha

    $11.33M cap room · $141.33M budget room · wing and frontcourt needs

    Only one wing and two bigs make this room actionable. AC Sparta Praha should be bidding on Billy Knight or Al Wood while keeping enough space for size.

  4. 4Arsenal

    $8.81M cap room · $158.81M budget room · one guard and one wing

    The roster shape demands perimeter help. Green is the aggressive answer; Mike O'Koren and Al Wood offer a more balanced two-player route.

  5. 5Valencia

    $6.17M cap room · $56.17M budget room · upgrades optional

    The cap figure is real, but the narrower budget cushion lowers the practical ceiling. Valencia can shop for value without manufacturing urgency.

  6. 6Real Madrid

    $5.36M cap room · $135.36M budget room · wing depth needed

    Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Wayne Cooper and several short-term additions already show a willingness to work the market. One credible wing would complete the logic.

  7. 7Chelsea

    $4.12M cap room · $129.12M budget room · guard and frontcourt needs

    Chelsea has less room than the leaders but two clear needs. Larry Smith would address the thinner frontcourt without forcing a long search for fit.

  8. 8Atletico Madrid

    $3.71M cap room · $133.71M budget room · guard and frontcourt needs

    James Donaldson's long re-signing and Robert Reid's one-year deal solved specific problems. The remaining room should now be used surgically, not symbolically.

  9. 9Inter Milan

    $3.62M cap room · $153.62M budget room · upgrades optional

    Inter already converted its leverage into Short, Ballard, Macy, Gilmore and Ray Williams. A lower rank now is evidence of work completed, not opportunity lost.

Control team: Aston Villa. Best patience: Juventus. Most urgent match of money and need: Arsenal. Quietly finished: Inter Milan.

The next board should reward action rather than unused capacity. Villa leaves FA2 with the most leverage, but two waves have shown that room depreciates when the right player disappears. The figure in the cap column stays the same; the number of useful ways to spend it does not.
