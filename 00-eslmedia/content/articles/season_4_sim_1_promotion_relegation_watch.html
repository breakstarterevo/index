November 1984 Race Watch: Nobody Has Room To Breathe

The first real lines are unforgiving: Chelsea and Benfica occupy Tier 1's bottom two, Real hold the second promotion place, and Barcelona have four pursuers.

Nina ValeNovember 1984

The rules are simple. Tier 1 relegates two teams. Tier 2 promotes two and relegates one. Tier 3 promotes one. The standings—not one hot month—define every line below.

Tier 1 Relegation: Chelsea And Benfica Are Below It
Current line: Chelsea, 3-6
Chelsea 3-6 | Benfica 2-7 | Palace, FL Fart and Bayern 3.0 GB from first

Chelsea are seventh and Benfica eighth, so they hold the two relegation places. The oddity is Chelsea's plus-3.4 season differential and plus-53 November: the results are bad, the underlying month was not. Benfica have no such comfort at minus-9.2 per game and three straight losses.

Crystal Palace and FL Fart are 4-4, while Bayern are 5-5; all sit 1.5 games above Chelsea by the standings gap. Palace's four-game losing streak creates the most immediate pressure. Chelsea control the escape route only by turning their improved play into wins.

Tier 2 Promotion: Real Hold The Second Ticket
Current line: Real Madrid, 5-2
PSG 6-1 | Real 5-2 | Villa 5-3 | Marseille 4-3

PSG and Real Madrid occupy the two promotion places. Aston Villa are only half a game behind Real and arrive on a three-game winning streak; Marseille are one game from the line. Ajax, at 4-5 and three games behind PSG, are close enough to monitor but outside the central pressure group.

PSG's plus-23.7 scoring margin makes their place the firmest. Real's direct 29-point win over Villa matters, but Villa's later win over PSG prevents the race from becoming orderly.

Tier 2 Relegation: A Tie With No Safety
Current line: Juventus, 2-6
Juventus 2-6 | Sheffield United 2-6 | Atletico 4-6

Juventus currently sit eighth, level with Sheffield. Juventus own the better immediate mood after two straight wins; Sheffield have lost five. Atletico are one game above both by the standings gap and therefore sweating, not safe.

The next pressure point belongs to Sheffield. Another loss paired with a Juventus win would turn a tie into separation and make the five-game slide materially expensive.

Tier 3 Promotion: Five Clubs, One Place
Current line: Barcelona, 6-2
Tottenham 0.5 GB | City, Inter and Monaco 1.0 GB | Valencia 1.5 GB

Barcelona lead, but Tottenham are half a game back and Manchester City, Inter Milan and Monaco are only one game behind. Valencia remain genuinely live at 1.5 games back. This is the league's broadest real race.

Tottenham's win at Barcelona and Inter's three-game streak give the chase substance. Sporting CP and Manchester United, both 1-8 and 5.5 games back, are not part of the promotion argument despite isolated upset wins.

November did not settle a line. It made every line visible.

December's decisive games are the direct ones: Villa against the promotion pair, Juventus against Sheffield, and any meeting among Tier 3's top five.
