November 1984 Tier 1 Power Rankings: Richmond Own The First Month

Richmond are the division's only trustworthy front-runner; beneath them, good records hide bad margins and one excellent Chelsea month hides a 3-6 hole.

Nina ValeNovember 1984

Trust starts with the full record, then asks whether the latest month explains it. AFC Richmond pass both tests: 7-1 overall, 6-1 in November and plus-159 for the season. No other Tier 1 club is within ninety-nine points of that differential.

This is the opening Season 4 board. Every position is a fresh ranking based on the first regular-season evidence: record and point differential first, then recent form, direct results and how repeatable each team's strengths appear.
