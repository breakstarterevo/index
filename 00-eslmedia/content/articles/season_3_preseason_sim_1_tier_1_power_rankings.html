October 1983 Tier 1 Power Rankings: Benfica Grab The First Chair

Benfica open Season 3 with the cleanest Tier 1 record, while Richmond, Milan and Bayern keep the preseason board from becoming settled.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Preseason Sim 1

A preseason board is a trust exercise, not a trophy presentation. Benfica start first because six games gave them the best combination of record, margin and late-sim direction in Tier 1.

The lens is simple: record first, point differential second, then the shape of the wins and losses. The players named here are the live preseason hooks, not a lifetime achievement roll call.

The concern is preseason volatility, especially if the best win over Chelsea becomes the peak instead of the baseline. Next trigger: repeat the control once the games start counting.

Benfica own the first read, but this board can move quickly because every team is still living on a six-game sample. The next sim decides whether this was signal or just the loudest opening paragraph.

Benfica have earned the first chair. Nobody has earned permanence.
