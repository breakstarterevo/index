Best Remaining Free Agents After FA2: Rickey Green Sets The Price

The second wave has one obvious starting guard, two credible forwards and a long run of specialists whose value depends entirely on the buyer.

Nina Vale1984 Offseason - After FA2

The remaining market requires a different evaluation standard. Two waves removed the stars and most broad bidding. After FA2, one clear starter remains at the top, followed by players whose usefulness depends on age, role and the exact hole a club needs to fill.

Rickey Green is the exception. At 27 and 124 overall, he is six points clear of the next available player. More importantly, he plays the position most obviously needed by Aston Villa, Arsenal, Tottenham and Monaco. His leverage comes from both quality and the lack of a comparable substitute.

How The Market Splits

Green should be evaluated alone because he can still start without qualification. Smith and Bailey are the frontcourt value branch. Knight, Wood and Wiley form the wing branch, with immediate quality declining as age value improves. Dunleavy, McGee, O'Koren, Sobers and Davis give guard-needy clubs several price points but not the same outcome.

The mistake after FA2 will be treating the ranking as a universal shopping list. AC Sparta Praha should value a wing and a big more than another guard. Sporting should do the opposite. Villa has enough room to solve both branches, but only if it stops waiting for an earlier-wave player to reappear. The best remaining target is the one who closes a real rotation hole at a price that still leaves the next move possible.
