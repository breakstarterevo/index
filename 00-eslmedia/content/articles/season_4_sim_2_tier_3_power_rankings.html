December 1984 Tier 3 Power Rankings: Barcelona Lead, Inter Look Stronger

Barcelona own the promotion place, Inter own the cleaner performance profile, and Tottenham keep winning games their margins say they should lose.

Nina ValeDecember 1984

Tier 3 has one promotion place and three different definitions of the best team. Barcelona have the best record. Inter Milan have the best differential. Tottenham have one fewer loss than Inter and a season margin of four. The board cannot solve that tension by pretending the standings and performance are the same thing.

Barcelona stay first because control of the actual line matters and a four-game winning streak answers the most immediate question. Inter move second because plus-91 is too substantial to leave behind a club surviving on close outcomes.

Tier 3 Trust BoardDecember 1984
  1. 1BarcelonaSTAYED NO. 1
    11-5 overall | 5-3 December | +100 | W4 | Paul Pierce

    Barcelona absorbed consecutive losses to Sporting CP, then closed with four wins and retained the only promotion spot. Paul Pierce's 28.3 points on 67.1 percent true shooting gives the run an efficient center rather than a lucky edge.

  2. 2Inter MilanUP 1 FROM NO. 3
    10-6 | 5-3 December | +91 | W2 | World B. Free

    Inter won December by 52 and the season by 91. World B. Free supplies 25.6 points in thirty minutes, while the defense can still close a month by holding Manchester United to 77. The two losses to Sporting and City prevent a takeover, not the argument.

  3. 3Tottenham HotspurDOWN 1 FROM NO. 2
    10-5 | 5-3 December | +4 | W3 | Doug Christie

    Tottenham are the inconvenient team. They won five December games and ended with three straight, yet were outscored for the month and are plus-four across fifteen games. At some point repeated close success becomes a skill; it has not yet become a cushion.

  4. 4ValenciaUP 2 FROM NO. 6
    8-7 | 4-4 December | +47 | L1 | Isiah Thomas

    Valencia's month was level, but the plus-26 margin and Isiah Thomas's 21.9 points with 9.9 assists say the team is healthier than the record. They are the first club outside the leading trio with a profile that could enter it quickly.

  5. 5MonacoDOWN 1 FROM NO. 4
    8-10 | 2-6 December | +24 | L3 | Andre Drummond

    A positive season differential is the only reason Monaco stop here. They lost six times in December and ended on three straight defeats while Andre Drummond continued doing the unglamorous work: 21 points, 12 rebounds and 2.2 blocks.

  6. 6Manchester CityDOWN 1 FROM NO. 5
    7-7 | 2-4 December | -6 | L1 | Kirk Hinrich

    City beat Barcelona and Inter during the month and still finished 2-4 with a minus-49 margin. That contradiction explains the ranking: a dangerous opponent whose best nights are too isolated to form a campaign.

  7. 7Sporting CPUP 1 FROM NO. 8
    5-11 | 4-3 December | -115 | L3 | Chris Bosh

    Sporting's 4-3 month included Barcelona twice and Inter once, an extraordinary improvement after November. Then came three straight losses by 18, 27 and 10. The rise is deserved; the recovery remains unfinished.

  8. 8Manchester UnitedDOWN 1 FROM NO. 7
    4-12 | 3-4 December | -145 | L2 | Josh Richardson

    United won three times, more than City or Monaco, and finished by losing 112-81 to Tottenham and 99-77 to Inter. The improvement was real enough to make the ending cruel and too brief to move them from last.

Control Team: Barcelona   Best Profile: Inter Milan   Trust Problem: Tottenham's margin   Danger Team: Valencia

There is no comfortable favorite. Barcelona own the line, Inter own the evidence and Tottenham own the habit of surviving. January will decide whether that is a three-team race or merely three ways of delaying separation.
