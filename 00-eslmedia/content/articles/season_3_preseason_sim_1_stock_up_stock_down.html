October 1983 Stock Report: Palace, Benfica And City Buy The Room

Crystal Palace, Benfica and Manchester City gained the most trust from the opening sim, while United, Monaco and Sheffield left repair work everywhere.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Preseason Sim 1

Stock reports are not polite. They are about who made the room louder and who made their own fans start bargaining with the word preseason. Season 3 already has both groups.

Stock Up
Crystal Palace
5-1 | +110 | L1 | Alex English

Crystal Palace bought real preseason credibility. The record is strong, the margin is visible, and Alex English gave the file a player spine at 26.3 pts, 8.5 reb, 3.7 ast. That does not crown anyone. It does mean the next conversation has to start with respect.

Benfica
5-1 | +58 | W3 | Moses Malone

Benfica bought real preseason credibility. The record is strong, the margin is visible, and Moses Malone gave the file a player spine at 25.0 pts, 11.0 reb, 3.0 ast. That does not crown anyone. It does mean the next conversation has to start with respect.

Manchester City
4-2 | +23 | W4 | Calvin Natt

Manchester City bought real preseason credibility. The record is strong, the margin is visible, and Calvin Natt gave the file a player spine at 36.2 pts, 12.2 reb, 3.2 ast. That does not crown anyone. It does mean the next conversation has to start with respect.

Stock Down
Manchester United
1-5 | -126 | L4 | Norm Nixon

This is not a disaster certificate, but it is a warning label. Manchester United left the sim with too much negative math and too little trust, even with Norm Nixon producing 21.5 pts, 5.2 reb, 8.7 ast. The next trigger is simple: stop giving the board easy reasons to doubt.

Monaco
1-5 | -50 | W1 | Purvis Short

This is not a disaster certificate, but it is a warning label. Monaco left the sim with too much negative math and too little trust, even with Purvis Short producing 29.3 pts, 9.0 reb, 3.0 ast. The next trigger is simple: stop giving the board easy reasons to doubt.

Sheffield United
2-4 | -38 | L1 | Steve Johnson

This is not a disaster certificate, but it is a warning label. Sheffield United left the sim with too much negative math and too little trust, even with Steve Johnson producing 22.7 pts, 8.2 reb, 2.8 ast. The next trigger is simple: stop giving the board easy reasons to doubt.

Preseason is not the verdict. It is the first receipt.

Crystal Palace, Benfica and Manchester City made people recalculate. Manchester United, Monaco and Sheffield United made people reread the fine print.
