December 1983 MVP Race: Chambers, Kiki And Tripucka Take The Ladders

The first full-season arguments now have records attached, and the ballot is getting less forgiving.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Sim 2

A month ago, production could carry almost the whole conversation. December makes that harder. The MVP race now has standings weight, margin weight and the simple question of whether the numbers are moving a team toward something real.

Tier 1 MVP Ladder
  1. 1Tom Chambers, AFC Richmond
    30.5 PPG | 5.6 RPG | .614 FG | +126
    Chambers takes the lead because Richmond are 14-1 and he is the scoring face of that machine. Thirty a night on elite efficiency gives the record a star name instead of just a system explanation.
  2. 2Magic Johnson, AFC Richmond
    20.6 PPG | 8.7 APG | .636 FG | +223
    Magic owns the best plus-minus number in the league and still looks like the player who gives Richmond its shape. He trails Chambers only because the scoring gap is real. The team-value argument is strong enough to flip this quickly.
  3. 3Joe Barry Carroll, AC Milan
    25.6 PPG | 8.0 RPG | 5.2 BLK | +74
    Carroll's raw scoring cooled from the first ballot, but five blocks a game on a second-place Milan team keeps him impossible to remove. He is still the most dramatic two-way box score in Tier 1.
  4. 4Adrian Dantley, Benfica
    28.6 PPG | 6.5 RPG | 5.6 APG
    Dantley has the complete offensive line, but Benfica's 3-5 December drags him down. The numbers remain top-three caliber. The team month does not.
  5. 5Bernard King, Bayern Munich
    29.9 PPG | 5.9 RPG | 5.5 APG
    King is still too productive to ignore and too buried in the standings to lift higher. Bayern are 4-11, which means every King case starts with a concession.
Tier 2 MVP Ladder
  1. 1Kiki Vandeweghe, Brighton
    39.7 PPG | 7.7 RPG | .574 FG
    Kiki still has the loudest scoring line in the league and Brighton are holding the second promotion seat. That combination matters. Palace have the better team, but no Palace player owns a single argument this sharp.
  2. 2Reggie Theus, Crystal Palace
    15.3 PPG | 8.5 APG | +184
    Theus is the organizer candidate. Palace are 16-2, and his passing is a major reason they look controlled instead of merely deep. The raw scoring keeps him second.
  3. 3Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Crystal Palace
    20.2 PPG | 7.5 RPG | 2.3 BLK | +177
    Kareem gives Palace the high-floor interior answer. He is not carrying the offense alone, but he is part of why Palace can absorb almost any matchup.
  4. 4Rolando Blackman, Juventus
    32.6 PPG | 5.6 RPG | 3.4 APG
    Blackman is the best challenger outside the promotion line. Juventus are close enough at 8-6 that a January push could make his scoring feel heavier.
  5. 5Larry Nance, Aston Villa
    31.6 PPG | 9.8 RPG | 2.1 BLK
    Nance has the two-way production, but Villa slipped to 7-9. He needs the standings to stop arguing against him.
Tier 3 MVP Ladder
  1. 1Kelly Tripucka, Paris Saint-Germain
    28.8 PPG | 4.5 RPG | +164
    Tripucka gets the top slot because PSG went 7-0 in December and now lead the tier. He has scoring, team context and the best plus-minus of the Tier 3 stars.
  2. 2Otis Birdsong, Barcelona
    30.3 PPG | 5.3 RPG | .433 3P
    Birdsong leads the tier in scoring and keeps Barcelona in the promotion chase. The gap is team momentum: PSG finished December like the race belonged to them.
  3. 3Brad Davis, Paris Saint-Germain
    19.4 PPG | 9.1 APG | +151
    Davis gives PSG the second ballot name because the attack is running through him cleanly. He is not just feeding Tripucka; he is stabilizing the whole month.
  4. 4Purvis Short, Monaco
    28.8 PPG | 5.9 RPG
    Short has the scoring to stay in the race, but Monaco's 4-4 December and three-game skid cost him the top spot.
  5. 5World B. Free, Inter Milan
    25.9 PPG | .432 3P | +91
    Inter are 12-4 and still right there. Free is the scorer on the second-place profile, which keeps him on the ladder even with PSG taking the headline.
The December ballot rewards production that changed the table, not production that merely survived it.
