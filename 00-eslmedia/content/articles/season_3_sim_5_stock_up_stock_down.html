March 1984 Stock Report: Milan Buy In, Atletico Hit The Floor

March rewarded clean surges and punished fake stability, which is another way of saying Milan and Atletico now live in different weather.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Sim 5

Stock reports are not standings tables with louder shoes. They are about direction. Who changed the way the league talks about them? Who made a good record feel sturdier? Who made a bad record feel like a warning label? March gave us plenty.

Stock Up: AC Milan

Six and oh. Plus eighty-three. W10. That is not a cute month. That is a team walking into the room and making second place sound like a threat instead of a mailing address. George Gervin is giving Milan scoring, Joe Barry Carroll is giving them structure, and Richmond finally have a chase team worth naming.

Stock Up: Brighton

Brighton went 6-1, moved to 27-10, and took control of Tier 2. Kiki Vandeweghe averaging 40.0 points per game is the kind of number that makes defensive game plans look like apology letters. The promotion seat is not theoretical anymore. Brighton are sitting in it.

Stock Up: Inter Milan

Inter are 27-9 with a W5 streak. PSG still lead Tier 3, but Inter are close enough to make every PSG loss feel loud. World B. Free gives them bite, and the chase profile looks much cleaner than it did a month ago.

Stock Down: Atletico Madrid

Atletico went 1-6 with a minus-96 month. That is not a dip. That is a trapdoor rehearsal. They are still five wins clear of Manchester United, but nobody should be comfortable losing by that much that often.

Stock Down: Real Madrid

Real Madrid are 11-26, on a five-game losing streak, and sitting in a Tier 1 relegation spot. Sidney Moncrief can score enough to keep a night respectable. He cannot, by himself, make this record respectable.

Stock Down: Valencia

Valencia fell to 14-23 after a 1-6 March. Isiah Thomas remains the name that makes you hesitate before writing them off, but March did not hesitate. It wrote them down in ink.

The buy list is easy: Milan, Brighton, Inter. The sell list is just as easy: Atletico, Madrid, Valencia. The uncomfortable part is that April may not be kind enough to let anyone call this temporary.
