Season 2 Preseason Tier 2 Power Rankings: Ajax Found The Fast Lane

Ajax open with five wins and the division's best margin, while Brighton and Juventus make the top of Tier 2 immediately competitive.

Nina ValeSeason 2 - Preseason Sim 1

Tier 2's opening sim offered the cleanest three-team conversation in the league. Ajax sit first because they combined five wins with a plus-51 margin. Brighton and Juventus are close enough to make the assessment provisional, but neither matched Ajax's complete opening schedule.

Nothing has been won in preseason. A prospective promotion division can still reveal which clubs already look organised for the chase.

1. Ajax - 5-1, plus-51

Ajax won five times, beat Manchester United twice, and added victories against Brighton, Chelsea and Monaco. Their single loss came by four points at Juventus, an away result that reads as competitive rather than alarming. The early message is clear: Ajax were ready to play useful basketball immediately.

2. Brighton - 4-2, plus-17

Brighton's margin is modest compared with Ajax, but four wins give them a firm second place. The home win over Marseille was their cleanest work; losses to Ajax and Manchester United show that there is still separation to recover against direct pressure. They look stable, if not yet commanding.

3. Juventus - 4-2, plus-8

Juventus beat Ajax and produced a strong 20-point road win at Marseille. They also lost at Monaco and to Manchester United, which makes their overall profile more volatile than Brighton's. Third is not a slight: it is recognition that the ceiling is obvious while the floor remains under examination.

4. Monaco - 3-3, minus-18

Monaco's win over Juventus earns attention, especially in a division where the top group already appears tight. The 28-point loss at Marseille explains why a level record does not earn a higher position. Monaco have evidence they can disrupt good teams; now they need evidence they can avoid the severe off-night.

5. Marseille - 2-4, plus-2

Marseille are the oddest team on the board. A losing record ordinarily pulls a team downward, but a positive margin means the losses were offset by authoritative wins, including the 130-102 defeat of Monaco. The board will be quick to move them if results begin matching their underlying balance.

6. Manchester United - 3-3, minus-32

United won at Juventus and Brighton, two results with real preseason value. Their minus-32 margin comes largely from losses that got away from them, most notably the 130-100 defeat at FL Fart. They are dangerous enough to beat anyone in this division and unstable enough to sit sixth until the extremes narrow.

7. FL Fart - 2-4, minus-5

FL Fart saved their sim from looking much worse by blasting Manchester United by 30 in the final appearance. That win matters, but four losses still place them below the group that found more repeatable ways to compete. Their next run is about deciding whether the United result was discovery or release.

8. Chelsea - 1-5, minus-23

Chelsea's one win came against FL Fart and gave them a foothold, but five defeats make the initial placement unavoidable. The margins were not as severe as the record suggests, which offers some hope; the immediate challenge is turning competitiveness into finished games before official pressure arrives.

Ajax earned the headline. Juventus and Brighton ensured they will not have a quiet route through this division.

This division may prove particularly resistant to first impressions. For now, Ajax are the only side that combined consistency, margin and opponent quality well enough to take the opening position without debate.
