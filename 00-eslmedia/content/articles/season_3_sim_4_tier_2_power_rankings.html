February 1984 Tier 2 Power Rankings: Palace Hold, Ajax Crash The Board

Palace still own the table, Brighton still own the second promotion place, and Ajax finally made the board respect the surge.

The Tier 2 board is a table-versus-form argument. Palace remain first because they still have the best full-season case. Ajax had the best month, but the sixth-place standing keeps the jump controlled rather than reckless.
