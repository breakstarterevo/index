December 1983 Tier 3 Power Rankings: PSG Take The Seat

The one-promotion race now runs through Paris, and December gave the rest of the tier a problem.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Sim 2

Paris Saint-Germain did not edge into first. They kicked the door open. A 7-0 month, a plus-21.9 December margin and a 48-point win over Manchester City make PSG the only honest No. 1 in Tier 3 right now.

Tier 3 Board - December 1983
  1. 1Paris Saint-Germain
    UP 2 FROM NO. 3 | 13-3 | +12.1 | W9 | Kelly Tripucka
    PSG are the form team in the league. Seven December games, seven wins, and no serious late-month wobble. Tripucka gives them the scorer, Brad Davis gives them the steering wheel, and the defense made the whole month feel unfair.
  2. 2Inter Milan
    UP 2 FROM NO. 4 | 12-4 | +8.6 | W4 | World B. Free
    Inter are not far away. They went 7-2 in December and still own the second-best record in the tier. The problem is that PSG beat the month so loudly that Inter's strong work became the quieter story.
  3. 3Barcelona
    DOWN 2 FROM NO. 1 | 11-5 | +4.9 | W2 | Otis Birdsong
    Barcelona are still a contender, not a collapse. Otis Birdsong leads the tier in scoring and the team went 5-3 in December. The issue is direct pressure: PSG beat them again, and the table now has Barcelona chasing.
  4. 4Monaco
    DOWN 2 FROM NO. 2 | 10-5 | +2.5 | L3 | Purvis Short
    Monaco were the November riser and the December warning. A 4-4 month with three straight losses knocks them from the promotion line conversation into the chasing pack. The talent is still real. The momentum is not.
  5. 5Sporting CP
    STAYED NO. 5 | 6-9 | -0.5 | W1 | Chris Bosh
    Sporting are the middle line: too competent to bury, too inconsistent to chase the top four. Chris Bosh gives them real production, but a 3-4 December did not change the season's direction.
  6. 6Tottenham Hotspur
    UP 1 FROM NO. 7 | 4-10 | -9.9 | W1 | Johnny Davis
    Tottenham move up because they keep beating Manchester City. The 96-75 win on 31/12 matters in a bottom-table fight, even if the wider profile still looks thin.
  7. 7Valencia
    DOWN 1 FROM NO. 6 | 3-11 | -9.4 | L1 | Isiah Thomas
    Valencia's December was one win and five losses. The Sporting win gave them a pulse, but the minus-11.5 monthly differential says the team is still losing too many stretches around its star guards.
  8. 8Manchester City
    STAYED NO. 8 | 2-14 | -10.6 | L5 | Kirk Hinrich
    City won twice, which is an improvement from November, but the ending was brutal: PSG by 48, Tottenham by 21. That is how a recovery month still finishes last.
Board Notes
Control Team: Paris Saint-Germain | Biggest Riser: Inter Milan | Trust Problem: Monaco | Panic Team: Manchester City

PSG now have the single promotion seat. Inter are close enough to make it uncomfortable. Barcelona and Monaco need January to stop the top two from turning this into a two-team race.

Tier 3 did not get cleaner. PSG just made the first answer obvious.
