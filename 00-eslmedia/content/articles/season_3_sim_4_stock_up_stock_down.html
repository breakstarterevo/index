February 1984 Stock Report: Richmond, PSG And Ajax Buy In

The month rewarded teams that matched record with margin and punished teams whose standings case no longer hides the damage.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Sim 4

February was not subtle. Some teams walked into the room with receipts. Some teams walked in with a small fire and asked us to call it atmosphere. No. We are not doing that.

Stock Up: AFC Richmond

Six wins. Zero losses. Plus-85. A 32-point beating of Bayern Munich and a 15-point close against Benfica. Richmond did not just protect first place; they made everyone else argue for second.

Tom Chambers gives them the scoring punch, Magic Johnson gives them the structure, and the record now looks like a verdict. If this keeps going, the only drama left in Tier 1 is below them.

Stock Up: Paris Saint-Germain

PSG went 8-1 with a plus-116 margin and beat Inter Milan 132-102. That is not a nudge. That is a billboard. Kelly Tripucka is making the MVP case easy to understand, and Brad Davis keeps the ball moving with adult supervision.

The Tottenham loss was annoying. It was not fatal. When you beat your closest promotion rival by 30, you are allowed one weird bruise.

Stock Up: Ajax

Ajax went 7-1, and yes, the table still says sixth. That is why this is stock up, not promotion coronation. There is a difference, and everybody needs to breathe.

Cliff Robinson gave Ajax a real basketball reason to believe, and the plus-63 month says the improvement was not a coin trick. Now they have to climb the actual standings.

Stock Down: Arsenal

One and five. Four straight losses. The coldest month in the package. Arsenal are not the worst team in Tier 3, but February made them look like the team most eager to join that conversation.

Stock Down: Marseille

Marseille went 2-6 with a minus-124 margin. Robert Parish can put up numbers until the lights go out; if the team is leaking that badly, the stock has to fall.

The danger is not Manchester United being good. The danger is United being just good enough while Marseille keeps sliding.

Stock Down: Atletico Madrid

Atletico had the month biggest win over Palace and the month biggest loss to Palace. That is not balance. That is confusion wearing a jacket.

A minus-81 month takes the shine off a mid-table record. The next sim has to be about defensive stability, because the board is done grading them on isolated moments.
