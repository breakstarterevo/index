November 1983 Review: Palace Run Hot And Richmond Stay Perfect

Crystal Palace owned the loudest month, AFC Richmond stayed unbeaten, and the lower-tier race tightened around Barcelona and Monaco.

Graham TrentSeason 3 - Sim 1

November gave the league its first honest map. AFC Richmond made Tier 1 look simple, Crystal Palace made Tier 2 look loud, and Tier 3 somehow became both crowded and unforgiving before anyone had time to pretend the table was still too young.

The useful thing about a first month is not certainty. It is pressure. Richmond are 7-0 with a plus-140 differential, which means the top tier now has a standard. Palace are 9-1 with a plus-157 mark, which means Tier 2 has a team everyone else has to explain. Barcelona, Monaco and Paris Saint-Germain are all sitting close enough in Tier 3 that the one-promotion format already feels rude.

Palace Made The Loudest Case

Crystal Palace did not just win games. They changed the tone of the promotion conversation. A 9-1 month with an eight-game winning streak is already a statement; doing it by 15.7 points per game makes it hard to file under early noise. The 141-101 win at Marseille was the clean headline, but the real story is how little oxygen Palace left for the rest of the tier.

Richmond Stayed Clean

AFC Richmond's month was less chaotic and more severe. Seven games, seven wins, no close-game dependence. The 158-118 handling of AC Sparta Praha was the biggest Tier 1 margin on Richmond's sheet, but the real value is that nobody has found the soft night yet. That matters in a tier where Bayern Munich are already 1-6 and Chelsea are only one result above the real trouble group.

Brighton Made Ajax Wear It

The single loudest score was Brighton 133, Ajax 90. A 43-point margin does not decide a season, but it does leave a mark. Brighton turned a strong 5-2 start into a promotion-line argument, while Ajax turned a bad month into a problem that cannot be hidden behind Cliff Robinson's talent. One result made both directions easier to read.

Tier 3 Is Already A Queue

Barcelona lead the line at 6-2, Monaco are 6-1, PSG are 6-3, and Inter Milan have the best point differential of the serious pack at plus-55. That is a lot of traffic for one promotion spot. The danger is obvious: a team can look like a favorite on Tuesday and a chaser by the weekend.

City, Ajax And Bayern Need Repairs

Manchester City's 0-7 month is the ugliest file in the package, and the losing streak matters more than any single box score. Ajax are 1-7 with a minus-100 differential. Bayern are only minus-28, which is less catastrophic, but a 1-6 Tier 1 start still puts them below the line. December does not need speeches from any of them. It needs wins.

November did not settle Season 3. It separated the teams with arguments from the teams with explanations.

The next sim is about proof under attention. Richmond and Palace have to defend authority. Brighton and Monaco have to turn strong positions into pressure. City, Ajax and Bayern have to show that November was a bad month, not a forecast.
