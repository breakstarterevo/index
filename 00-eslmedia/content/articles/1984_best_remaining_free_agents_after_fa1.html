Best Remaining Free Agents After FA1: English Sets The Price

Stars remain at wing, younger answers remain at guard and center, and the next phase will punish clubs that confuse choice with depth.

Nina Vale1984 Offseason - After FA1

The remaining market is strong at the top and less forgiving beneath it. Clubs still have options, but not interchangeable ones. Each branch has a player who sets its price.

Franchise-Changing Wings

Alex English, SF, 28: The best current player available and the safest primary-scoring bet. His market fits Crystal Palace's explicit wing need, but any team with room should at least test the price.

Purvis Short, PF, 25: A 134 overall and 138 potential profile gives him both immediate impact and term value. He can justify a longer commitment than most of the board.

Greg Ballard, SF, 27: A prime-age wing with a 131 overall profile. The English pursuit will shape his market; clubs that miss at No. 1 may quickly turn Ballard into the next bidding contest.

Rolando Blackman, SF, 23: Youth is the separator. A club building on a longer timeline can prefer Blackman's runway to an older player's certainty.

Lead Guards

Kyle Macy, PG, 24: The cleanest age-and-position fit on the board. Inter Milan, Real Madrid, Atletico Madrid and Aston Villa all list guard depth among their needs and all have meaningful cap space.

Rickey Green, PG, 27: The next reliable lead-guard option. His value rises sharply if Macy signs early because the remaining point-guard tier lacks the same blend of prime age and established quality.

Gus Williams, PG, 28: A credible alternative for clubs seeking immediate creation without paying the very top of the market.

Centers: Certainty Or Timeline

Artis Gilmore, C, 32: The win-now choice. Age narrows the ideal contract, but his 128 overall profile can still stabilize a contender's interior immediately.

James Donaldson, C, 24: The long-horizon choice. Donaldson matches Gilmore's 127-level impact closely enough that a rebuilding or ascending club should value the extra eight years.

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, C, 35: A shorter-term specialist bet. The fit is a contender that can protect the minutes and needs veteran interior quality more than future value.

The Next Decision

Atletico Madrid and Aston Villa can bid at the top without choosing only one roster need. Inter Milan can do the same with even greater overall budget freedom. Crystal Palace has the clearest one-position match. Marseille, Valencia and Real Madrid have enough room to matter but less capacity to recover from a miss.

The board is not shallow yet. It is concentrated. English controls the wing market, Macy controls the younger guard market, and the Gilmore-Donaldson split defines the big-man decision. Clubs that understand which question they are actually answering will find value. Clubs that wait for every price to fall may discover that the right player has already gone.
