Super Cup Group Stage Wrap-Up: Bayern Took The Tiebreak

Three clubs finished 7-1, but Bayern's cleaner score profile, Milan's scoring punch, and United's volatility made the bracket feel more dangerous than settled.

Graham Trent Super Cup group stage review

The Super Cup group stage ended with the kind of table that appears orderly only until one reads it twice. Bayern Munich, AC Milan, and Manchester United all reached 7-1. Benfica, Juventus, and AFC Richmond sat one game behind. Then came the familiar middle-class anxiety: five-win teams with strong claims, four-win teams with awkward evidence, and a three-win cut line that made Chelsea's positive differential feel like a small joke played by the standings.

Bayern get the first word because they earned the least complicated one. Their 7-1 record came with the competition's best margin at +16.8 and the best defensive profile among the elite, allowing only 95.0 points per game. Milan were almost as persuasive, finishing 7-1 with a +16.0 differential and 114.8 points per game. United matched the record but arrived differently, scoring 114.3 and allowing 107.6, which is less a warning sign than a reminder that their games tend to leave fingerprints on the furniture.

The Top Line
Bayern, Milan, United: same record, different mood
All 7-1 | Bayern +16.8 | Milan 114.8 PPG | United 114.3 PPG
The table gives Bayern the cleanest resume, Milan the sharpest offensive headline, and United the most interesting problem. United's group stage included enough scoring to scare anyone and enough leakage to keep the room from getting comfortable.
The Chasing Six

Benfica, Juventus, and AFC Richmond each finished 6-2, which is a lovely place to be until one realizes none of them gets to pretend this bracket is gentle. Benfica's case is balance: 111.1 scored, 96.6 allowed, Moses Malone leading the whole competition at 35.4 points per game. Juventus were more compact, 109.3 to 101.4. Richmond, meanwhile, led the field in scoring at 117.4 points per game and made the familiar Richmond argument: perhaps not quiet, rarely tidy, usually dangerous.

Below them, FL Fart, Ajax, Crystal Palace, Brighton, and Real Madrid all finished 5-3. That is where the competition gets mildly rude. Crystal Palace had the best differential of that group at +8.6. Real Madrid were +6.8. Brighton were +5.4 and scored 116.9 a night. FL Fart and Ajax were closer to neutral on margin, but both finished on winning streaks. As a group, they look less like lower seeds than teams with one bad week and one good matchup away from making the bracket unpleasant.

The Games That Stuck
Bayern 129, Manchester United 81
April 15 | 48-point margin
This was the loudest result of the group stage and the neatest explanation for why Bayern's top seed feels more substantial than a tiebreaker. United still finished 7-1, but a 48-point loss to the team above them is not exactly fine print.
Atletico Madrid 95, AC Milan 94
March 20 | One-point finish
Milan's only blemish came by one point, which is both a compliment and an irritation. It kept them from the cleanest possible group stage and gave Atletico a result that looked better than their final 2-6 record.
The Bottom Of The Table

Valencia went 0-8 and finished at -24.6 per game, which is not a slump so much as a full audit. Barcelona's 1-7 line looked better only because the offense still had teeth at 107.0 points per game. The strangest exits were Chelsea and Atletico Madrid. Chelsea missed the cut at 3-5 despite a positive differential. Atletico finished 2-6 despite beating Milan. The Super Cup was not especially interested in sympathy.

The Individual File

Moses Malone was the group stage's blunt-force headline, leading the competition at 35.4 points per game for Benfica. James Donaldson topped the rebound board at 12.1 for Atletico Madrid, Larry Drew led assists at 10.1 for FL Fart, Darwin Cook averaged 3.1 steals for Brighton, and Tree Rollins blocked 4.6 shots a game for Bayern. That last number feels especially relevant now. The top seed also brought the rim deterrent.

The group stage did not produce a mystery favorite. It produced several reasonable lies. Bayern look strongest. Milan look explosive. United look combustible. Benfica look balanced. Richmond look loud. And somewhere in the bracket, at least one five-win team is preparing to make a higher seed explain itself at length.
