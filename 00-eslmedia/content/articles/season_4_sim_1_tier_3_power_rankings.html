November 1984 Tier 3 Power Rankings: Barcelona Lead A Five-Team Sprint

Barcelona own the line, Tottenham own the closest pursuit, and Inter's three-game finish has made the middle of the table impossible to dismiss.

Nina ValeNovember 1984

One promotion place has attracted five credible claims. Barcelona are first at 6-2. Tottenham are half a game back. Manchester City, Inter Milan and Monaco are all one game from the lead. This board rewards control, but it cannot pretend control is secure.

This is the opening Season 4 board, with every club ranked fresh from the first regular-season sample with no movement comparison to an earlier list.
