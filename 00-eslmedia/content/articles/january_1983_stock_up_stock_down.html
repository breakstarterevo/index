January Stock Report: Bayern, Juventus And AC Sparta Praha Buy The Room

Bayern Munich, Juventus and AC Sparta Praha rose hard in January, while Valencia, Crystal Palace and Benfica made their problems impossible to ignore.

Damon CrossJanuary 1983

Stock reports are supposed to be simple. Did you make the league believe more or less in you than it did a month ago? January gave us three clean buys and three names that need to stop asking for patience.

Stock Up: Bayern Munich

Stop pretending Bayern are merely solid. They went 7-2, closed W6, and beat Atletico Madrid by 52. Bernard King gives them the scoring, but the point is larger: Bayern's best month did not look like noise. It looked like a team finding its level.

Stock Up: Juventus

Juventus went 6-1 and finished W5 after spending too much of the season looking like a problem someone would eventually have to explain. Kareem Abdul-Jabbar is still Kareem, which helps. But the real buy signal was the 107-87 win over FL Fart, a promotion-race result with a little muscle behind it.

Stock Up: AC Sparta Praha

AC Sparta Praha are not flashy. Fine. The table is not a fashion show. A 6-3 month, plus-61 margin, and a 101-68 road win at Bayern Munich give them the kind of evidence that cannot be laughed away. The L2 finish is why this is stock up, not stock soaring.

Stock Down: Valencia

Valencia went 1-5. That is the sentence. Isiah Thomas is good enough to make every game watchable and not quite enough, by himself, to cover a minus-48 month. The only win came by three at Barcelona. The losses had too much air in them.

Stock Down: Crystal Palace

Palace beat Richmond and Real Madrid and still make this list. That tells you how much damage sits around those wins. A 2-6 month, minus-79 differential, and 30-point loss at AC Milan are not the profile of a team solving itself. Darrell Griffith can score. Palace need more than moments.

Stock Down: Benfica

Moses Malone is putting up an MVP case while Benfica are putting up distress signals. That is the problem. They went 2-5, lost by 35 to Atletico, and could not convert Malone's production into safety. A win at Real Madrid keeps the story alive. It does not fix it.

Bayern and Juventus changed their arguments. Valencia, Palace and Benfica are still asking their stars to cover team-level problems.

February will tell us whether the risers bought something permanent. The fallers do not need a narrative refresh. They need wins.
