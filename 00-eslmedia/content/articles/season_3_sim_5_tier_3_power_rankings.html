March 1984 Tier 3 Power Rankings: PSG Lead, Inter Apply Heat

PSG still own the trust board, but Inter's closing streak and Monaco's record make the one-promotion race less comfortable.

Tier 3 remains a one-promotion tier, so trust has to start with who can actually finish first. PSG have the table lead and the margin. Inter have the form. Monaco have the line pressure. Barcelona have the talent. That is enough mess for one board.
