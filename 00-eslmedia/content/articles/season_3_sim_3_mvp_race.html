January 1984 MVP Race: Moses, Kiki And Tripucka Control The Ballots

The January ladder is where raw scoring stops being enough and team context starts deciding who actually owns the race.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Sim 3

Stop pretending this is only a points race. It is not. January gave the league three different MVP arguments: Moses Malone as the best two-way engine in Tier 1, Kiki Vandeweghe as the loudest statistical force in Tier 2, and Kelly Tripucka as the scorer keeping PSG in the Tier 3 driver's seat.

Tier 1 Ballot
  1. Moses Malone, Benfica - 27.5 points, 10.4 rebounds, 2.8 blocks, and Benfica just went 6-2. That is value with a table attached.
  2. Joe Barry Carroll, AC Milan - 26.5 and 8.7 keeps Milan's contender case alive.
  3. Tom Chambers, AFC Richmond - Richmond's best scorer still has the best team context, even if the gap narrowed.
  4. Bernard King, Bayern Munich - 30.5 a night and the Richmond upset give him oxygen despite the record.
  5. Jim Paxson, Chelsea - 28.5 points on a club trying to stay clear of real trouble.
Tier 2 Ballot
  1. Kiki Vandeweghe, Brighton - 39.8 points per game with a 33.5 PER. There is no quiet way to say that.
  2. Larry Nance, Aston Villa - 31.3 and 10.1 keeps Villa from fading into the middle.
  3. Rolando Blackman, Juventus - 31.7 points and a 6-2 month moved Juventus into the promotion argument.
  4. Robert Parish, Marseille - The numbers are heavy, but the team context is now dragging the case.
  5. Mark Aguirre, Brighton - 29, 6.8 and 4.2 gives Brighton a second star-level case.
Tier 3 Ballot
  1. Kelly Tripucka, Paris Saint-Germain - 28.8 points with PSG still right at the top of the race.
  2. Otis Birdsong, Barcelona - 30.5 a game and enough team success to make the scoring matter.
  3. Purvis Short, Monaco - 29.7, 6 and 3; the caveat is Monaco losing a little control.
  4. World B. Free, Inter Milan - Inter went 6-1, and Free's scoring makes the run believable.
  5. Lewis Lloyd, Arsenal - A strong individual profile on a team that still needs more wins.

The biggest riser is Blackman because Juventus changed the race around him. The biggest statistical case is Kiki because 39.8 per game is absurd. The hardest case to judge is Bernard King, because his scoring is elite but Bayern are still living too close to the wrong end of Tier 1.
