December 1984 Stock Report: Real Madrid Bought The Whole Month

One perfect run changed a promotion race; elsewhere, brief recoveries and spectacular collapses made December less generous than the standings suggest.

Damon CrossDecember 1984

Eight games. Eight wins. Plus-165. Real Madrid did not improve their stock in December; they cornered the market and charged everybody else admission.

This report rewards a month that changed what a club can reasonably expect from its season. It punishes a month that changed the same expectation in reverse. One upset is not a recovery. One ugly loss is not a collapse. Seven straight losses will qualify.

Stock Up
Real Madrid — Promotion Became The Minimum

Real went 8-0 and never won by fewer than nine. Sidney Moncrief is scoring 27.7 points on 66.7 percent true shooting with a plus-12.7 margin. PSG remain excellent, but Madrid changed the question from whether they can promote to whether anyone can stop them winning the tier.

Inter Milan — The Margin Is No Longer Background Noise

Inter finished 5-3, plus-52 and closed by holding Manchester United to 77. Their season margin is plus-91, almost twenty-three times Tottenham's despite nearly identical records. World B. Free gives them scoring; the team profile gives the promotion argument weight.

Benfica — Two Richmond Wins Demand Attention

Benfica beat the league's best team twice and went 4-3. Moses Malone remains the obvious reason to believe. The minus-38 monthly margin is the reason not to celebrate too quickly, but a club that began December as an easy last-place answer now has a real escape route.

Stock Down
Aston Villa — Seven Straight Is A Structural Alarm

Larry Nance averaged 32.2 points, nine rebounds and 2.3 blocks. Villa lost seven straight anyway and were outscored by 134 in December. When a star performs that well and the team collapses that completely, the problem is not bad luck around the edges.

Crystal Palace — One Milan Win Cannot Hold The Roof

Palace beat AC Milan by 19 and still went 2-5 with a minus-53 margin. The month also included a 45-point loss to Richmond. Darrell Griffith preserves the offensive possibility; the defense keeps turning possibility into a brief interruption.

Manchester City — The Upsets Refuse To Connect

City beat Barcelona and Inter, then finished 2-4 and minus-49. That is a highlight reel, not a run. Every time the ceiling appears, another double-digit loss arrives before the club can build beneath it.

Real Madrid made a strong month look inevitable. Aston Villa made an elite individual season look helpless.

January will reveal whether Benfica's Richmond formula travels and whether Inter can turn superior margins into the actual Tier 3 lead. Villa do not have the luxury of waiting for a larger sample. Seven straight losses have already supplied one.
