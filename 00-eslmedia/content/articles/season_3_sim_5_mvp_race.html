March 1984 MVP Race: Kiki Has The Number, Richmond Has The Problem

The raw scoring crown belongs to Kiki Vandeweghe, but the ballot still has to answer what to do with Richmond's control and PSG's efficiency.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Sim 5

Stop pretending this is just a scoring table. If it were, Kiki Vandeweghe would grab the trophy, the press room would applaud, and everyone could go home before the lights warmed up. Forty points a night for Brighton is ridiculous. It is also only the start of the argument.

1. Kiki Vandeweghe, Brighton

Kiki is averaging 40.0 points on the team sitting first in Tier 2 at 27-10. That matters. It is not empty noise. Brighton went 6-1 in March, and every defense knows the first, second, and third problem. The reason he leads the board is simple: the production is absurd and the team is winning.

2. Tom Chambers, AFC Richmond

Chambers is not winning the counting-stat shouting match, but he is the cleanest Tier 1 candidate on the league's cleanest team. Richmond are 33-4. Chambers is at 29.3 points per game, Magic Johnson is still bending the game beside him, and the whole thing looks unfair in a way voters tend to remember.

3. Kelly Tripucka, Paris Saint-Germain

Tripucka has the Conference League version of the argument: 29.4 points per game, a 30-8 PSG record, and a plus-12.3 team differential. The concern is that PSG share more of the machinery with Brad Davis than the headline lets on. Fine. The machine is still first.

The Dangerous Names

Bernard King leads Tier 1 scoring at 30.5, but Bayern Munich are 15-22. Sidney Moncrief is at 29.8 for Real Madrid, and Real Madrid are stuck in the relegation places. Moses Malone has 28.6 points and 10.9 rebounds for Benfica, while George Gervin's 28.5 points now come with Milan's W10 surge. Those are not footnotes. They are the next fight.

The ballot today is Kiki, Chambers, Tripucka. The ballot tomorrow could change if Milan keep sprinting or Richmond starts splitting credit too evenly. But right now, the case starts with a scorer nobody has solved and then asks whether team control can catch him.
