February 1984 Tier 1 Power Rankings: Richmond End The Debate

The trust board starts with the team that went unbeaten, but Chelsea and Milan changed the chase while Benfica lost its grip.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Sim 4

Tier 1 has a standings leader and a trust leader, and in February they were the same team by a wide margin. The board still weighs full-season profile first, but Richmond's latest month removed most of the caveat language.
