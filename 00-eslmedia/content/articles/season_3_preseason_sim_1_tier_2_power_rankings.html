October 1983 Tier 2 Power Rankings: Palace Own The Loudest File

Crystal Palace and Aston Villa both went 5-1, but Palace pair the record with a plus-110 margin that makes the first trust call clear.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Preseason Sim 1

A preseason board is a trust exercise, not a trophy presentation. Crystal Palace start first because six games gave them the best combination of record, margin and late-sim direction in Tier 2.

The lens is simple: record first, point differential second, then the shape of the wins and losses. The players named here are the live preseason hooks, not a lifetime achievement roll call.
