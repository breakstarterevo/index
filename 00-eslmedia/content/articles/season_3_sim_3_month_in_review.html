January 1984 Review: Valencia Run Hot And Richmond Finally Look Human

January did not crown new champions, but it changed the temperature around Valencia, Brighton, Juventus and a suddenly mortal Richmond.

Graham TrentSeason 3 - Sim 3

January was the month the league stopped looking tidy. AFC Richmond are still the best Tier 1 team on the board, Crystal Palace still own Tier 2 by the full-season table, and Paris Saint-Germain still carry the cleanest Tier 3 resume. Yet the month belonged to the teams that disturbed those neat lines.

Valencia were the loudest of them. A 7-2 January with a plus-76 differential did not move them near the actual promotion line, but it did move them from background noise into the stock-up file. Isiah Thomas did not need to be perfect; he needed the club to look organized, fast, and dangerous enough to make the teams above them uncomfortable.

Brighton Made The Month's Biggest Statement

Brighton's 146-95 demolition of Marseille was the bluntest single result of the sim. Kiki Vandeweghe's scoring case has been loud all season, but this was about more than one scorer. Brighton went 5-2, posted a plus-97 margin, and reminded Tier 2 that Palace are not alone in having promotion-level force.

Richmond Are Still Great, Just Not Untouchable

Bayern Munich's 113-109 upset of AFC Richmond was the sharpest top-tier result because it put a second loss on Richmond's record and made the leader look human. Richmond still finished January 5-2 and still have the best season-long shape in Tier 1. The difference is that the room no longer has to speak about them as if the answer is predetermined.

The Race Lines Got Messier

Juventus went 6-2 and Atletico Madrid went 6-3, turning the Tier 2 promotion line into a live fight instead of a Palace-Brighton procession. Inter Milan answered PSG with a 6-1 month and a one-point win at Monaco. Tottenham and Manchester City, meanwhile, did not merely lose ground; they lost the ability to make their seasons sound theoretical.

January did not settle the league. It made the next sim matter more.

The month leaves a useful kind of disorder. Richmond can still restore the old hierarchy. Palace can still pull away. PSG can still finish the job. But Valencia, Brighton, Juventus and Inter have all made sure the next board cannot just copy the last one, even if only some of them are truly on the race line.
