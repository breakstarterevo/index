October 1983 Review: Benfica And Palace Make Preseason Loud

Benfica took the first Tier 1 chair, Crystal Palace owned the largest margin profile, and Tier 3 opened as a three-team argument.

Graham TrentSeason 3 - Preseason Sim 1

Season 3 opened with enough preseason evidence to make the first rankings useful and just enough chaos to keep them honest. Benfica took the Champions League room at 5-1, Crystal Palace posted the biggest margin profile in the second tier, and Inter Milan left Tier 3 with a narrow but real first claim.

The cleanest number belongs to Crystal Palace: 5-1, +110, and a 141-104 win over Aston Villa that still sits as the biggest blowout of the sim. Palace did not just win often. They won with enough force to make the promotion conversation start with them.

Tier 1 Starts With Benfica

Benfica did the most difficult preseason thing: they made their best result and their overall file tell the same story. The 129-100 opening win over Chelsea gave the board a headline, and the later wins over Richmond and AC Milan gave it substance. Moses Malone and Adrian Dantley make the attack feel too stable to dismiss.

Tier 2 Is Already Heavy

Aston Villa also went 5-1, and that matters. The difference is Palace's plus-110 compared with Villa's plus-73, plus the direct 37-point Palace win. Ajax sit one game back with Cliff Robinson making a noisy early player case, while Manchester United already have a repair file at 1-5 and -126.

Tier 3 Refuses To Separate

Inter Milan, Barcelona and Manchester City all finished 4-2. Inter lead this review because their plus-30 is the best of the group, but Barcelona are one point behind on differential and City closed on a W4 streak. Calvin Natt's 36.2 points and 12.2 rebounds per game make City the team nobody should treat like a quiet third.

The preseason did not settle Season 3. It did give every tier a first argument.

The next sim is about confirmation. Benfica need to prove the first chair is more than a hot week. Palace need to make their margin travel. Inter need to create distance before Barcelona or City turn a thin edge into a chase.
