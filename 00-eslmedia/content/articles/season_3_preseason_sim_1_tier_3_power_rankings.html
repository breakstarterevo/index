Nina ValeSeason 3 - Preseason Sim 1

A preseason board is a trust exercise, not a trophy presentation. Inter Milan start first because six games gave them the best combination of record, margin and late-sim direction in Tier 3.

The lens is simple: record first, point differential second, then the shape of the wins and losses. The players named here are the live preseason hooks, not a lifetime achievement roll call.
