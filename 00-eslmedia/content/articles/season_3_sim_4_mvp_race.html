February 1984 MVP Race: Kiki Is Untouchable, Moses Has A Problem

The numbers still matter, but February made team context too loud to ignore in all three MVP rooms.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Sim 4

Stop pretending every MVP race is the same argument. Kiki Vandeweghe is not in a normal argument. He is averaging 39.1 points on .703 true shooting for Brighton, and Brighton owns a promotion spot. That is not a case. That is a locked door.

Tier 1 Ballot

1. Tom Chambers, AFC Richmond. Chambers gets the top Tier 1 slot because 29.5 points, .654 true shooting, and a plus-9.5 floor come attached to a 25-3 team that just went unbeaten. The case is production plus winning, which is the shortest route to the chair.

2. Moses Malone, Benfica. Moses has the heavy numbers: 27.2 points, 10.7 rebounds, 2.7 blocks, .619 true shooting. The problem is Benfica just went 2-4 and closed L3. That matters. MVP is not a museum for great stat lines.

3. Joe Barry Carroll, AC Milan. Carroll is still absurd defensively at 4.6 blocks with 25.7 points on .573 shooting. Milan closing W4 keeps him alive. He needs AC Milan to keep climbing.

4. Sidney Moncrief, Real Madrid. Thirty points a night with defense belongs on the ballot. A 10-20 Real Madrid record keeps it from becoming a winning argument.

5. Magic Johnson, AFC Richmond. The raw scoring is lower, but 20.1 points, 8.8 assists and a plus-12.6 rating explain why Richmond hums.

Tier 2 Ballot

1. Kiki Vandeweghe, Brighton. Thirty-nine a game. Elite efficiency. Promotion position. That is the cleanest case in the league.

2. Larry Nance, Aston Villa. Nance gives Villa 32.0 points, 10.0 rebounds and 2.1 blocks. He is doing star work for a team that is still below .500, which is why he is second and not first.

3. Mark Aguirre, Brighton. Aguirre at 30.1 points is the second reason Brighton are not merely a one-man show. That also limits his path to first because Kiki is standing in the same room.

4. Rolando Blackman, Juventus. Blackman has 31.8 points and Juventus remain third. The L4 close hurts, but the pressure on Brighton keeps the candidacy alive.

5. Robert Parish, Marseille. Parish is averaging 26.0 and 13.3, but Marseille going 2-6 forces a harsh question: how much value survives when the team keeps sinking?

Tier 3 Ballot

1. Kelly Tripucka, Paris Saint-Germain. Tripucka leads the best team in the tier at 29.8 points with a plus-10.5 profile. PSG are 26-6. This is the adult answer.

2. Otis Birdsong, Barcelona. Birdsong brings 30.5 points and keeps Barcelona in the chasing pack. He needs the table to tighten before the case can overtake Tripucka.

3. Brad Davis, Paris Saint-Germain. Davis is the engine: 19.7 points, 9.1 assists, .417 from three. The only reason he is third is that Tripucka is the sharper scoring face of the same machine.

4. Purvis Short, Monaco. Short has the numbers and Monaco closed W3, but the team is still 7.5 games back. The path exists. It is narrow.

5. World B. Free, Inter Milan. Inter are closest to PSG, and World B. Free gives them the shot creation to make the race real. The 30-point PSG loss is the bruise on the ballot.

The biggest riser is Chambers. The strongest statistical case is Kiki. The player whose numbers need context is Moses. Simple as that.
