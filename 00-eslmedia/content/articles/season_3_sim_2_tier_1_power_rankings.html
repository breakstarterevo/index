December 1983 Tier 1 Power Rankings: Richmond Stay Clear, Milan Move Second

Richmond finally lost, but the rest of Tier 1 still has to chase their shape.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Sim 2

The Richmond loss did not open Tier 1 as much as it confirmed who is allowed to ask the next question. AC Milan get that right because they beat Richmond, held second place, and kept the best non-Richmond differential in the tier. Everyone else is either trying to regain trust or trying to stay clear of the trapdoor.

Tier 1 Board - December 1983
  1. 1AFC Richmond
    STAYED NO. 1 | 14-1 | +14.4 | W2 | Tom Chambers
    The perfect record is gone, not the authority. Richmond went 7-1 in December, still lead by four games, and answered the Milan loss by beating Real Madrid the next night. The only concern is whether the defense has softened from November's absurd level.
  2. 2AC Milan
    UP 1 FROM NO. 3 | 10-7 | +3.2 | L1 | Joe Barry Carroll
    Milan take second because they own the Richmond win and still have the best chase profile. Joe Barry Carroll gives them rim protection no other contender can copy. The 122-94 loss to Benfica is the reminder that this remains a volatile No. 2.
  3. 3Benfica
    DOWN 1 FROM NO. 2 | 9-7 | +3.5 | W1 | Adrian Dantley
    Benfica's season number is still good enough for third, but the 3-5 December changed the tone. Beating Milan by 28 showed the ceiling. Losing to Chelsea by 27 showed why the board no longer treats them as Richmond's first threat.
  4. 4Chelsea
    UP 1 FROM NO. 5 | 6-6 | +1.5 | L1 | Jim Paxson
    Chelsea are .500 with a positive differential and enough top-half evidence to sit fourth. The problem is availability of dominance: they beat Benfica by 27, then lost to Milan by 28. That is a profile, not a verdict.
  5. 5FL Fart
    UP 1 FROM NO. 6 | 8-9 | -1.5 | L1 | Larry Drew
    FL Fart fixed the November damage better than anyone near the bottom. A 6-3 month moves them out of panic and into the middle, with Larry Drew still giving the offense enough passing to survive rough stretches.
  6. 6Real Madrid
    DOWN 2 FROM NO. 4 | 6-9 | -6.5 | L5 | Sidney Moncrief
    Real Madrid's December was the warning. Two wins, six losses, and a five-game skid mean the November rise has gone cold. Sidney Moncrief keeps them organized, but the table is starting to sound louder than the roster.
  7. 7Sheffield United
    STAYED NO. 7 | 5-12 | -9.0 | W1 | Bill Cartwright
    Sheffield are still in danger, but December was less destructive than November. The defense remained shaky, but the margins improved and the Chelsea win gives them something to point at.
  8. 8Bayern Munich
    STAYED NO. 8 | 4-11 | -4.6 | W1 | Bernard King
    Bayern are the strangest last-place team because the point differential is not hopeless. The record is. At 4-11, with only a 3-5 December, the talent argument keeps getting answered by the standings.
Board Notes
Control Team: AFC Richmond | Biggest Riser: FL Fart | Trust Problem: Benfica | Panic Team: Real Madrid

Richmond are still separate. Milan are the first real challenger. The bottom half is now less about talent and more about who can stop bad weeks becoming identity.

Richmond lost once. Tier 1 still spent December proving how hard they are to catch.
