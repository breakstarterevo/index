January 1984 Stock Report: Valencia, Benfica And Juventus Buy In

January rewarded teams that turned form into leverage and exposed the clubs whose records no longer have a defense.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Sim 3 stock report

There are good months, and then there are months that change how everyone has to talk about you. Valencia had the second kind. Benfica and Juventus had versions of it too. Tottenham, Manchester City and Manchester United had the other kind, the kind where the room goes quiet because the evidence is rude.

Stock Up: Valencia
7-2, plus-76, hottest team of the sim

Valencia did not win the season in January. They did something more useful for a mid-table Tier 3 team: they made themselves impossible to dismiss. Isiah Thomas now has a real race attached to his name.

Stock Up: Benfica
6-2, plus-92, Tier 1 chase team

Moses Malone and Adrian Dantley dragged Benfica back toward the top of the Tier 1 trust board. That is what a contender response looks like.

Stock Up: Juventus
6-2, plus-73, W4 streak

Rolando Blackman gave Juventus more than points. He gave them a promotion argument with a backbone.

Stock Down: Tottenham Hotspur
0-8, minus-206

This is not a dip. This is a collapse wearing a schedule.

Stock Down: Manchester City
0-7, minus-90

The youth story can wait. The scoreboard is screaming now.

Stock Down: Manchester United
0-7, minus-59

United are not unlucky. They are losing games and losing the benefit of the doubt at the same time.

The next report starts with whether Valencia and Juventus can keep their receipts. The teams at the bottom need something more basic: proof of life.
