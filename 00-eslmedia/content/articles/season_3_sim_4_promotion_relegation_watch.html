February 1984 Race Watch: PSG Stretch, Tier 2 Starts To Split

The rules are simple, and February made several lines cleaner: two down from Tier 1, two up and one down from Tier 2, one up from Tier 3.

Nina ValeSeason 3 - Sim 4

Race status starts with the table. Monthly form can explain pressure, but it does not move the line by itself. After February, the cleanest race fact is that PSG lead the only Tier 3 promotion slot, while Palace and Brighton hold the two Tier 2 promotion places.

Tier 1 Relegation: Real Madrid And Sheffield Below The Line

Tier 1 sends two teams down. Right now those teams are Real Madrid at 10-20 and Sheffield United at 9-20. Bayern Munich are the first escape target at 11-19, which makes this race real rather than symbolic.

Real Madrid still have Sidney Moncrief production, but a 2-5 February left them seventh. Sheffield closed W2, which matters, but they still have the worst record in the tier. Bayern are not safe enough to relax after a 3-4 month and a 32-point loss to Richmond.

Tier 2 Promotion: Palace And Brighton Control It

Tier 2 promotes two teams. Crystal Palace lead at 23-8. Brighton hold second at 21-9. Juventus are third at 17-13, 5.5 games back of Palace and four games behind Brighton in the race that matters.

Ajax went 7-1, but sixth place at 13-18 keeps that surge in the stock-up category, not the promotion category. The table is blunt here. Brighton are the line team, Juventus are the nearest serious chaser, and Ajax need more than one hot month to enter the real pressure group.

Tier 2 Relegation: United Still Own The Trapdoor

Only one team goes down from Tier 2, and Manchester United remain there at 8-23. Marseille are closest at 12-19 after a 2-6 month, so the pressure is not gone. It is just still heavily tilted toward United.

United's February was better than the record suggests: 3-4 with a plus-34 margin. That keeps the door open. Marseille's minus-124 month keeps the door visible.

Tier 3 Promotion: PSG Lead, Inter Chase

Tier 3 has one promotion spot. Paris Saint-Germain own it at 26-6. Inter Milan are the only close chaser at 22-7, 2.5 games back. Barcelona, 6.5 games behind PSG, need help before this becomes a true three-team line.

PSG's 8-1 month matters because it came with direct separation. The 132-102 win over Inter was the sort of result that changes how the next month is framed. Inter are not out of control, but they are no longer level with the leader.

The next pressure point is simple: Bayern versus the bottom two in Tier 1, Brighton versus Juventus in Tier 2, and whether Inter can take back any of the ground PSG just claimed.
