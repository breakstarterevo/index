December 1983 Stock Report: PSG, FL Fart And United Rise

December rewarded teams that changed their direction, not teams that merely stayed interesting.

Damon CrossSeason 3 - Sim 2

Stock is not the standings. Stock is what changed. Palace and Richmond are still excellent, but December's biggest movement came from teams that altered the way their tier has to talk about them.

Stock Up: Paris Saint-Germain
Tier 3 | 7-0 December | +21.9

This is the easiest call in the package. PSG entered December in the chase and left it in first. The 115-67 win over Manchester City was excessive in the useful way: it made the whole tier see the new leader clearly.

Stock Up: FL Fart
Tier 1 | 6-3 December | 8-9 overall

FL Fart did not become a contender, but they repaired the dangerous part of their season. A winning month moved them away from the bottom two and made the November slide look less permanent.

Stock Up: Manchester United
Tier 2 | 3-5 December | 136-93 over Atletico

United are still in trouble, but this is a relative market. They created separation from Ajax and produced the kind of result that can matter later if the relegation race tightens.

Stock Down: Benfica
Tier 1 | 3-5 December | Down to third

Benfica still have Adrian Dantley and Moses Malone, but December made their contender case less clean. The 122-94 Milan win was followed by enough damage that they no longer look like Richmond's obvious first challenger.

Stock Down: Monaco
Tier 3 | 4-4 December | L3

Monaco did not collapse, but they lost the shine. A three-game losing streak in a one-promotion tier is expensive, especially when PSG and Inter both spent the month accelerating.

Stock Down: Atletico Madrid
Tier 2 | 2-5 December | -15.3

Atletico's December moved them from uncomfortable middle to survival-adjacent. The 43-point loss to Manchester United was the kind of result that changes the way a table reads.

PSG bought the headline. Benfica and Monaco bought January pressure.

The next month will tell us which December moves were real identity changes. PSG have to defend first. FL Fart have to prove the climb was more than fixture relief. Benfica and Monaco need a clean response before their seasons start sounding smaller than their talent.
