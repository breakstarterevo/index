November 1985 Tier 2 Power Rankings: FL Fart Win The Month, Inter Own The Line

Three clubs have separated from the division, but the best monthly record, strongest margin and official promotion places belong to different arguments.

Nina ValeNovember 1985

Tier 2 has produced a useful disagreement. FL Fart went 7-3 in Sim 1. Inter Milan lost once and outscored opponents by 78. Ajax won five of seven and closed on three straight victories. Only two promotion places exist, so one excellent November is already insufficient.

The board rewards the full body of work, then tests it against margin, opponent quality and the next pressure point. FL Fart's extra wins matter; Inter's fewer opportunities and superior efficiency make the top two nearly interchangeable.

Tier 2 Trust BoardNovember 1985
  1. 1FL FartUP 2 FROM NO. 3
    7-4 overall | 7-3 Sim 1 | +72 | Larry Drew

    The strange name is no longer the story. Seven wins are. Larry Drew is averaging 25 points and 10.9 assists, and the 120-86 win over Bayern showed how quickly control becomes separation. One concern remains obvious: six of the seven wins came at home.

  2. 2Inter MilanSTAYED NO. 2
    6-1 | 5-1 Sim 1 | +81 | W2 | World B. Free

    Inter have the best winning percentage, the best margin and a 4-0 road record. They also lost at home to one-win Atletico Madrid. That upset costs them the top spot by a fraction; it does not change the conclusion that Inter own the cleanest promotion profile.

  3. 3AjaxDOWN 2 FROM NO. 1
    6-2 | 5-2 Sim 1 | +70 | W3 | Andre Iguodala

    Ajax are punished for being third-best in a top three this strong. Andre Iguodala is scoring 29.8 points on 71.7 percent true shooting, and the three-game closing streak is a direct answer to the 15-point loss at FL Fart. Promotion quality is present; promotion space is not.

  4. 4Aston VillaUP 1 FROM NO. 5
    4-5 | 3-5 Sim 1 | -15 | W2 | Larry Nance

    Larry Nance gives Villa 32.2 points, 10.7 rebounds and 2.7 blocks a night. November still finished below water. The final two wins create a credible recovery; until Villa defend well enough to make Nance's work decisive, fourth is a ceiling rather than momentum.

  5. 5AC Sparta PrahaUP 3 FROM NO. 8
    4-5 | 3-5 Sim 1 | -47 | L3 | Steve Johnson

    Steve Johnson's 35.1 points and 10.9 rebounds prevent AC Sparta Praha from becoming ordinary. Three straight losses and a minus-52 Sim 1 margin prevent his production from becoming an argument for fourth. The preseason scoring explosion was real; the structure around it remains unresolved.

  6. 6MarseilleUP 1 FROM NO. 7
    3-4 | 3-3 Sim 1 | -25 | Robert Parish

    Marseille beat AC Sparta Praha by 25 and nearly balanced the sim margin, then surrendered 142 at FL Fart. Robert Parish supplies rebounding and rim protection. The perimeter has not yet protected his work from track meets.

  7. 7Bayern MunichDOWN 1 FROM NO. 6
    2-6 | 2-5 Sim 1 | -82 | L4 | Tree Rollins

    Bayern beat AC Sparta Praha by 30, then closed with four losses and suffered a 43-point defeat at Inter. That is not inconsistency; it is one good answer surrounded by a month of worsening questions.

  8. 8Atletico MadridDOWN 4 FROM NO. 4
    1-6 | 1-5 Sim 1 | -54 | L2 | Johnny Moore

    Atletico own the upset of the month, a 123-112 win at Inter. It is also their only win. Johnny Moore's 10.9 assists and 3.3 steals can disrupt good opponents; the league's worst record says disruption has not become a repeatable offense.

Control Team: FL Fart   Best Profile: Inter Milan   Pressure Team: Ajax   Panic Team: Atletico Madrid

The next board may be decided away from home. FL Fart must travel, Inter must treat the Atletico loss as information rather than comedy, and Ajax must keep winning without waiting for either rival to make room.
